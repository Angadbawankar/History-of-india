Classical India (500 BCE - 1200 CE)

Classical India (500 BCE – 1200 CE) was a period of significant cultural, political, and intellectual development in the Indian subcontinent. This era saw the rise and fall of powerful empires, the flourishing of philosophy, science, and the arts.

Medieval India (1200–1750 CE)

Medieval India was a period of political transformation, cultural synthesis, and economic growth, marked by the rise of Islamic sultanates, the Mughal Empire, and regional kingdoms.

Colonial & Modern India (1757-Present)

The British colonial period in India began with the Battle of Plassey (1757) and ended with India’s independence in 1947. Over these 190 years, British rule transformed India’s economy, society, and politics, leaving a lasting impact.
